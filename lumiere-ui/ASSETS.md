# Asset provenance

## Editorial hero

- Saved project path: `dist/assets/skincare-portrait.png`.
- Created with the built-in Imagegen tool for this project; no reference image was used.
- The image is a fictional adult model and is not a user's photo, a before/after result, or diagnostic evidence.
- Original generated file was retained; the selected asset was copied into this project.

Exact generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial skincare portrait for an elegant L'Oreal-inspired personal skincare dashboard, not a full webpage. Create a photorealistic square beauty photograph of an adult East Asian woman about 25 years old, dark brown hair neatly swept back, bare shoulders above a neutral cream camisole, natural visible skin texture with faint freckles, minimal makeup, calm gentle confident expression, looking toward camera, her right hand delicately resting on the side of her neck near her jaw. Composition: head and shoulders, face occupies upper middle-right of frame, show entire top of head with breathing room, warm beige studio backdrop. Sophisticated French beauty editorial, warm soft directional window light, realistic pores, soft shadows, subdued warm ivory and light tan color palette. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no graphics, no bottles or products. Beautiful human naturalism, not plastic or excessively retouched. A single full-bleed photograph.

## Vector assets

The favicon, line icons, chart graphics, and moisturizer concept packaging were authored directly in SVG for the interface. The moisturizer is explicitly labeled concept packaging and must not be presented as a real product listing. Brand text is used only for the requested L’Oréal-inspired hackathon concept.

## Typography

DM Sans is loaded through Google Fonts when available. Georgia and system sans-serif fallbacks keep the application usable without font-network access. No external photograph is embedded.
